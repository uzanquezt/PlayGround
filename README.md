# PlayGround

**Sporefall** — a hand-drawn mushroom-knight platformer that runs right in the browser.

The grove is rotting from the roots. Fight slimes, bats, and thornlings, gather glowing spores, forge three tiers of living armor, and cut a path to the crown through three chapters and their guardians. Every level is hand-built, every sprite is drawn procedurally on canvas, and all music and sound effects are synthesized on the fly — no assets, no build step, no dependencies.

- **Play:** open `index.html` through the preview server, or run `./start.sh`.
- **Controls:** `A`/`D` or arrows to move, `Space` to jump, `J` to strike, `Shift` to dash, `E` to interact. Touch controls appear automatically on phones and tablets.
- **Goal:** gather spores to forge all three armor tiers, then defeat the three guardians. Lantern checkpoints keep your progress when you fall.
